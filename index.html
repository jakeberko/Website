About

I enjoy solving backend engineering problems that require understanding how systems work, why they fail, and how they can be improved. There's something rewarding about taking a complex system and leaving it more reliable than I found it.

That curiosity led me to Amazon Web Services (AWS), where I completed two software engineering internships before joining full-time as a Software Development Engineer. I worked on secure cloud platforms, modernized production code-signing workflows serving 2,000+ users, improved backend performance, and built tooling that helped engineers deploy and manage software more efficiently.

What I enjoy most isn't just writing code, it's solving difficult engineering problems. Whether improving software quality, modernizing legacy systems, or building scalable backend services, I enjoy creating solutions that make systems more reliable and easier for other engineers to work with.

I'm excited to continue growing as a Backend Software Engineer, contribute to product-focused teams, and keep learning from engineers who enjoy solving challenging technical problems together. Long term, I'd like to expand into full-stack development while continuing to build software that creates meaningful impact.
